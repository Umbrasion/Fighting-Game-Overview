I find it funny how much Skullgirls seems to just lurk. I've had non-fighting game friends who have Skullgirls in their Steam library for some random reason several years ago. I feel like that was me as well, I can't even remember when or why I bought my copy. Who knows what it is.
This character select was the first time I had full access to the original assets of the game, thanks to a program called SkullMod. I went pretty accurate with it, though maybe a little too accurate, since it can look a bit squished when you're not viewing it in fullscreen (so, most of the time). This used to be my most proud character select, but as time's gone on, there's a fair few others that I'm overall more proud of. This one isn't bad per se, just lacks some of the polish I would give these later down the line.
Having access to the full files of Skullgirls, I was able to get the music from the game itself. Most of the time, I just record it myself and cut it down to get an accurate and perfect enough loop. I've noticed a problem with those music loops though; for some reason, some browsers don't loop the audio perfectly and there's a noticable gap in between loops. Not sure how to fix it, let alone if I even can in the first place, but it works on some browsers so oh well.
