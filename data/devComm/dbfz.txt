At the time of writing, Daima Goku just came out and I had to redo all of what I had already done since the character select got all moved around to accomodate the new character. Perhaps I'll be looking forward to more of that in my future... just the way it goes. It's cool to see the game still getting supported though. I'm no Dragon Ball fan, but I do find it a little funny how the new character is just Goku but again. I guess they just wanted to push Daima more.
This was the first character select where I really delved into HTML maps... they're very convenient for character selects with more angular icon shapes that tend to overlap with each other. If I didn't use maps, you'd notice some overlapping of the icons when you went to hover over them around the corners. This isn't the first game I used them with, but with so many characters, I ended up having to measure a lot of borders.
Thanks to forcing me into a rework from Daima Goku's release, I ended up coming up with a new system for resizing character icons when the window changes sizes. It's way better and more efficient than what I was doing before, and hopefully it'll serve me well in future pages. It should help things look better on smaller resolutions (I don't own any computers/monitors that aren't 1920x1080, so I just have to hope). Sometimes I think about how it'd look in more varied settings, like if it's extremely wide or very scrunched. But I have to imagine that scenario is unlikely... hopefully.
