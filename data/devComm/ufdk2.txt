Kyanta is probably one of the more played kusoge, since it's free on Steam. I personally happened across it while scrolling fighting games on Steam with a friend just to see what would pop up. We ended up having a hoot at the absurd story, so worth the find I suppose.
Whenever you move the cursor, it plays a random cursor sound effect. In-game, these would play based on which direction your cursor would move, but at the time of making it, I didn't feel like making a system for recognizing which direction you've moved, so I just made it random. I was told at some point that it was funnier this way. If they say so.
Funny story: You may be wondering how I managed to get a clear shot of the background when, if you've played the game, you'll know that you never have a view of the background without the other elements in the way. The answer is somehow not asset ripping. For some reason that I can't explain, when you take a screenshot of this game using Steam's screenshot feature (pressing F12 while in-game), it will take a screenshot that includes only the background, and none of the UI. This gave me a shockingly easy way to get the background for this game, and it was done purely by accident. I'm still baffled that you can do that.
