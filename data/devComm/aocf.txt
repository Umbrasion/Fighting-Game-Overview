Personally speaking, AoCF is one of, if not my favorite fighting game of all time, simply for how unique it is. Air combat with three lanes, allowing you to "jump" up and down, plus how unique and strange the characters in this game can get with their kit... it's pretty fun. The Touhou fighting games are pretty niche though, and if you know anything about them, you're more likely to have heard of Hisoutensoku over this one, so it's a niche game within a niche subsection of a divisive genre. But sometimes that's what makes fighting games so interesting.
Originally this character select looked way different and much more off-model. One of my biggest headaches I encounter while working on these is never finding anything character select related on Spriter's Resource. You could find a million different sprites on there, but nothing related to the character select. So sometimes I have to make do, and I originally did on this one. It was a lot harder though, because of how many moving parts and layers exist in this one, to the point where, as mentioned, it was very off-model. Luckily I managed to find a way to rip the sprites myself and now it looks extrememly accurate. Maybe overly so.
One of the downsides of being accurate is that it's hard to make these look universally good. You'll notice a lot of things that look fine if you're viewing the site in fullscreen, but are slightly off when viewing it normally. It's noticeable in a fair few of these (and the dev commentary on other pages will likely mention those), for instance on this one, the "selected" character portrait is misaligned if you're not viewing in fullscreen. Just something to deal with though. As long as it looks at least pretty good, then that's fine with me. Mostly.
