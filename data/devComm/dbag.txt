I happened to hear about this game's Steam release from a random Tweet I stumbled across. I'd love to have the exAGAIN version instead, but I've always had headaches with arcade emulators and even if I didn't, it still seems like it'd be hard to find a ROM of it in the first place. So Additional Gain it is.
Saw this game described as "Arcana Heart for fujoshis" in a review on Steam and that's a pretty accurate description. It's made by the same dev team, so a lot of the systems will feel familiar if you know Arcana Heart. There's some neat characters, and a decently well-balanced cast too. Haven't played much at the time of writing, but I've certainly had fun messing around with it.
Pretty much every asset (besides the portraits) are from screenshots I took of the game myself. I did try to find the actual portraits that were used in-game (the ones used here are taken from Mizuumi and are different portraits from the actual ones), however the only sprite rips I could find were of the in-battle sprites, and I couldn't find a method of ripping the assets myself. Go with what you're given, as it were.
