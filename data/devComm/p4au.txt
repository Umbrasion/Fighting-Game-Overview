I've always appreciated Persona's stylish nature, it has some very appealing UI and art direction, and the music is highly enjoyable, so it's fun to see that style in a fighting game. It's one of those games where you've got some fun experimental character mechanics -- if you know anything about this game, you've probably heard about Iori. Those kinds of unconventional characters are some of my favorites.
P4AU can be aptly credited as the reason this site exists in the first place. Originally this site started as a supplemental resource for a small tournament series I hosted that was done with a circle of friends and friends of friends, and started as basically a Dustloop clone. After realizing that was a silly idea, I wanted to change it to something more flashy, something that acted more as a "hook" to let you find interesting fighting games or even get into them in the first place. I already had tried my hand at making character selects that used the icons from the original games, one of them being P4AU, which was by far the most intricately made of the bunch. I took the concept I had with P4AU and decided to go the full nine yards into what you see here today.
Even now, this is still probably the character select with some of the finer tuning. With such an irregular design, you can't exactly do things conventionally. There's a lot of stuff to point out -- the angled portrait areas to prevent external clipping, the actual icons, the way the names pop in... hopefully all this effort is enjoyed by whoever is visiting.
