Kill la Kill -IF is my first arena fighter. I generally don't play arena fighters, but they can be fun. I'm apparently bad at Rock-Paper-Scissors though. I peronally class arena fighters as fighting games; it's just a subgenre, really. Whether you like them or not is, of course, subjective. But I digress. I know of this game's existence purely because of Alpharad, back when I used to watch him. Then I came across it later and decided to get it.
The character portraits were an interesting thing to find. There aren't really "portraits" in-game as much as they are just a render of the character staring straight ahead. On Mizuumi they used portraits that I assumed were just reference images from the actual show, so I checked the official website for this game... and found out that those portraits on Mizuumi were the same ones on the official site. So that's just what I'm using here.
I mostly talk about the images and formatting on these, so here's a tidbit about the audio: for any game that doesn't have audio rips on Spriter's Resource, I'll generally just go into the game, turn the game audio to zero, turn whatever sound category I need up to max, and record the sounds myself. It can take a while, especially in games like this where each character has a dialog line when you select them. Don't get me started on games that do that with even bigger rosters than this... this game's on the easier end of the spectrum in that regard.
