DNF Duel has a pretty contentious reputation. Some people like it, a lot of people didn't. Personally, I wish it had done better, and it's an enjoyable experience to me. I'm aware that as someone who tends to enjoy fighting games of all shapes and sizes, and am not a competitive player, my opinion does not hold much water, but I like recognizing the fun in games like these, especially in a genre that I feel strongly about.
Coding things in (presumably) very inefficient ways is my forte. As a wise man once said: "As long as it works, that's what matters." In this case, it's the cursor, due to the fact that the cursor has four different looks depending on which part of the selection it's at. There's a lot of ways that I've done things on here that are done in a pretty awkward manner, and while over time I've cleaned up and refined some of the code that's more egregious, I'm sure there's a lot you could nitpick. Comes with the territory, though.
I try not to use stuff from other sites as much as I can. Usually the only thing that I borrow from sites like Dustloop or Mizuumi are character portraits. There is one other thing I borrow from elsewhere sometimes: color palettes, especially for games with 3d models instead of spritework. One funny thing about that though is you get a variation of quality; for some reason the color portraits for DNFD on Dustloop are oddly squished. Who knows why.
