MBTL was my first French-Bread game, so I ended up playing AACC later down the line. I have no real preferance for the old versus the new -- maybe if I played them more religiously then I'd have a greater opinion. I can understand the gripes with the roster though; the last two characters added being from Fate instead of Tsukihime is a funny decision. I've never engaged in either home series though, so I can't really say I have a horse in this race.
One of the things I would like to improve on for this one is the background. There are a few games with this problem, but sometimes they just don't have a particularly good view of the background, so I just have to recreate the basic colors. The actual background has a few moving parts, but even a still shot would work. Makes it more visually interesting.
The character lines on hover was fun to add. In my quest for pinpoint accuracy, even the smallest details do not get glossed over. Unless they're highly impractical or tedious to add. I ended up timing how long it takes for the voicelines to play (which, if you're curious, is 5 seconds for the first line to play, and 10 seconds after the first line starts for the second line). I always like when a character reacts to being chosen, even if I don't speak Japanese and have little idea of what they're saying.
