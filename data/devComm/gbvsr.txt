Granblue is one of my favorite series of all time, and as a fighting game enthusiast, you'd best believe I'm happy that there's a fighting game for it. I wish more series had fighting game spin-offs. I digress though; Granblue has always had a special place in my heart ever since I got really into Relink. I actually played the original GBVS before Relink, but that was when I was trying out a scattered bunch of FGs with a friend of mine. I'm a regular on the original mobile game as well, been playing since around June of 2024. Obviously, GBVSR is one of my favorite fighting games. Is that bias talking? Whatever you think.
Any game made on Unreal is luckily very easy to rip assets from. Pretty much everything you see here is a direct rip, and whenever I get to do that, it bumps up the accuracy of how these look tenfold. Even made the difficulty rating stars into uncap stars. The EX toggle is a fun addition as well. I wonder if there'll be more EX characters in the future... it'll be an AACC all over again for me.
A little behind-the-scenes: there's a feature on the info display where it can go all the way up to six stars, for characters that are extremely technical and demanding, with a very high skill floor and ceiling. The uncap stars have that compatability too; the 6th star is the full Transcendence star. It will more than likely go completely unused though, GBVSR is not a particularly technically demanding game, so it doesn't reach the crazy difficulty that some other games do.
