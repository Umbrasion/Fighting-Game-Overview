I once saw AACC described as a game with "too many characters", which I've always found funny. It's pretty cool that there's so many options, and different ways to use the same character. I can only imagine the dev time to make and balance all those differences; though, with the moon styles just being variants of the same character, it's not like you have to make a whole new set of sprites.
This character select is certainly one of my prouder ones. Everything all comes together nicely and doesn't end up looking weird. Plus you get to listen to a top-tier character select theme. The background test is a particular favorite, that was transcribed as accurately as possible from my own observations. The display names are also accurate to the game, down to "Powerd Ciel" and "Hisui&Kohaku" as opposed to "Powered Ciel" and "Hisui & Kohaku". The main things that could be improved is the background, as it's just a still shot early in the actual transition, and the icons changing on hover (which is hard to do without the original sprites).
Due to the moon style system, there's quite a lot of descriptions to write across the cast, since I have to write three per character. Unfortunately I don't have any fighting game friends who really play this game, let alone own it, so for now it's all on me to write them...
Funny story: The Japanese text for each character's name was taken from the Type-Moon wiki, with the sole exception of Akiha Vermilion. The wiki listing of her Japanese name didn't include the character "主", so I ended up having to ask a friend of mine who does translation work for the character in text after failing to find it on my own.
