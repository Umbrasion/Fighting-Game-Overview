I remember playing UNI2 some time ago, when I was only just getting into fighting games and wasn't very good at them. I had the misconstrued idea that UNI2 was this high execution, precise and difficult FG purely because the person I played with mained Vatista, and I assumed that every character was as difficult as her. I was, of course, wrong. Nowadays I deeply enjoy the game, as with most of the games on this site.
One thing I like in this game is all the little text that you can find everywhere. Small, lengthy captions underneath short titles, giving a very verbose description of whatever the title is communicating. Or the little character descriptions that appear behind them on the character select. They're fun little details that just add some flair to the presentation. The character descriptions are taken from Mizuumi, as I am unaware of any other place they appear, though the ones on Mizuumi seem to not be updated to UNI2, as there are some differences between them and what I can see in-game. You work with what you got, though.
The background of this character select isn't actually the same one that appears in-game, but instead, it's the one that apears on the title screen. There's not any good opportunities to get a good look at the character select background, so I had to make do with a place I could. It looks pretty similar, at least.
