I always find these niche fighting games most people have never heard of well after they've come out through random content I stumble across or find my way to, and this is no exception. It's quite fun, in my own personal opinion. And it has Iori, which is funny. Fun fact: This game was the testing grounds for the current way that information is displayed when you select a character. Originally, instead of a pop-up window, the info would be put on the P2 side (since the portrait went on the P1 side, it left some convenient empty space). That did, however, create the problem that depending on how big the character select or the P2 space was, it could scrunch up the info and make it hard to read, or in some cases, just overlap with other things, making it unreadable. So it was redone. It does leave the P2 side pretty empty though...
