It's a bit of a shame this game is pretty widely ignored at this point. Obviously I can understand why -- $60 price tag, bad netcode, etc. -- but I personally really enjoy this game and for all it does wrong, the simple joy of being able to do absurdly stupid things no matter the character you choose is an unmatched fun. Though maybe that's just the casual side of me speaking.
Respect to the dev team for still making content on this game. Shizuku just released at the time of writing, and there's still another DLC character to go. I can't imagine that there'll be another season of characters, but it sure would be fun. Hope the dev team is at least enjoying working on the game, 'cause sales are definitely not high and likely won't be picking up, unless a miracle happens.
