About the Creator

Hi! I'm Umbrasion, the creator and maintainer of this website. I've been an avid lover of fighting games ever since I started playing them and I deeply enjoy any and all fighting games across the genre. Every game on this site is one I personally own, so each aspect of the site is made out of pure passion for each and every game.

While I am not a top player by any means, I've always wanted to do something fun and engage with the fighting game community, so as someone who is familiar with coding and putting together websites, I ended up working on this! It's been slowly chipped away at and evolving since mid-2024, and I'll certainly be at it for a while. The intent has been to put something visually fun and appealing out there for people to appreciate and to maybe get some on-the-fence people to check out fighting games, or get existing fighting game players to try out some new games!

The website is always open to suggestions, so feel free to let me know anything that you think could be added or changed!
