Future Plans

A short list of plans for the site in the future.

A "main menu" for each game

Instead of just going into each game and being immediately greeted with the character select, instead you can look at more baseline information about the game, such as availability, price, general gameplay feel, singleplayer options, and other info of that kind. From there you can travel to the character select menu as you like.

Update to description information

Character information is mostly a placeholder, whether it be the information to be filled out, or the few characters that actually have things written about them. The information is intended to be stuff that's introductary and easily digestible to beginners, so it should ideally be things that can help sell you on a character, like how easy they are, how they play, etc. There are plans to offer less gameplay focused information, such as character colors and lore.

Special move lists for characters

Self-explanatory.

Mobile layouts

While fitting the different layouts on a screen they were not built for is not exactly easy, it's important to make it accessible and easy to navigate for everyone.

Audio fixes

Currently some music and sound effects are widly different volumes between games.
